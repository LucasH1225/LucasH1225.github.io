Explore

The same records, seen six ways.

Everything you write in Capture is saved once. Explore reads those records back by person, by meal, by workout, by day or as a list. Nothing here is a separate thing to keep up to date.

These short loops use made-up records and are based on the Mac prototype's Explore views. The prototype is not available to download.

People

Who you spent time with

A map that starts from you. Select a person to see the moments you recorded together, or switch to a plain list.

  • Links come only from things you wrote down.
  • No closeness scores. Appearing in the same note doesn't make two people friends.
Meals

What you ate, day by day

A food diary with a daily calorie view. Open a day to see which meals make up its number.

  • Estimated calories are labelled as estimates.
  • Meals without calories are counted as missing, not as zero.
Workouts

Movement you recorded

Minutes per day from the sessions you logged, with each session listed underneath.

  • It totals what you recorded. It isn't a complete activity count.
Journal

The day, in your own words

Entries keep what you wrote. A note about yesterday is filed under yesterday, even if you wrote it today.

  • No mood scores, and no lesson required.
  • Undated pages say "No date" instead of getting a guessed one.
Timeline

Everything, in order

Plans and recorded moments together, newest day first. Filter to events, journal entries or things with no date.

Shopping

One list for things to buy

Items are grouped by category with any quantity you mentioned. Mark them bought; bought and cancelled items stay findable.

  • A Reminder is created only if you ask to be reminded.

Where the records come from

Every view above is filled from Capture: typed notes, voice-message transcripts, photos, and items you drag in from your day. Try Capture on the overview page.
